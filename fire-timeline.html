What to enter as Current Net Worth

✓ Include

  • Investment property equity (value minus loan)
  • Share portfolio / ETFs / managed funds
  • Cash savings and offset account balance
  • Family trust investable assets
  • Super — but read the note below

✗ Exclude

  • Primary place of residence equity — you live there, it generates no income
  • Personal property (cars, jewellery, etc.)
  • Unvested shares or options
  • Business goodwill (unless you intend to sell)
⚠ Super and early retirement: Super is only accessible at your preservation age (currently 60 for most people). If you're targeting retirement before 60, you need to fund the gap years from non-super assets alone. The conservative approach is to run two scenarios: one excluding super (to confirm you can fund retirement until ~60), and one including it (to confirm long-term sustainability). If you include super, mentally note that those funds aren't available until preservation age — your non-super assets carry all the weight in the early years.
